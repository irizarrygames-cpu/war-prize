War Prize — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

War Prize is a card game you play against other people online. This page explains exactly what the game stores about you, why, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be skipped.

What the game stores

  • The username you choose. Pick anything you like — it does not have to be your real name, and we would rather it was not.
  • Your password, scrambled. The password itself is never stored. It is put through PBKDF2-SHA256 150,000 times with a random salt, and only that result is kept. Nobody, including us, can read your password back out of it.
  • Your game progress. Trophies, coins, level, matches played, which card backs and tables you own, and your daily challenges.
  • Your friends list — the usernames of players you added, and any requests waiting.
  • Sign-in tokens, so you stay signed in on your own devices and do not have to type your password every time. Up to five at once.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, age or birthday.
  • It does not know or record where you are.
  • It has no advertising of any kind.
  • It has no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party trackers.
  • It does not sell or share anything about you with anyone.
  • It has no chat. Players can only send each other a small fixed set of emoji reactions chosen by the game, so nobody can send anyone a message.

Where it is kept

Accounts and progress are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon. The game itself runs on Render. Both are service providers that hold the data on our behalf; neither uses it for anything else. Traffic between your device and the game is encrypted with HTTPS.

Deleting your account

You can delete everything yourself, from inside the game. Open Profile → Delete My Account and enter your password. Your account, your progress and your place on the leaderboard are removed immediately and permanently, and your username goes back into the pool for someone else to use. There is no waiting period and nothing is kept afterwards.

If you cannot get into your account, contact us at the address below and we will delete it for you.

Children

War Prize is suitable for children, and we have kept it that way on purpose: no ads, no chat, no purchases with real money, and nothing collected beyond a username and how far you have got in the game. Usernames are screened when an account is made so that rude ones cannot be used.

Because the game does not ask for an email address, a birthday, or anything else that identifies a real person, there is nothing here that could be used to find or contact a child outside the game. If you are a parent or guardian and would like an account removed, use the deletion steps above or write to us and we will take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Anything significant will be announced in the game itself.
